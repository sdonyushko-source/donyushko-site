The process

From day one
to launch and beyond

01

Discovery

The more we know, the better the outcome. Target users, the experience you want to create, standout features and the challenges you face — no detail is too small.

From there we outline and prioritise features, define the technologies, estimate timelines and map out next steps.

02

Wireframing & prototyping

Our UI/UX designers explore the user journey in depth and map every step in a wireframe — surfacing key features and places where users could get stuck.

You finish this stage with prototypes that show how everything will work and what needs to be built.

03

Design

If the wireframe is the skeleton, this is where it comes to life. We integrate your brand identity — or help you create one that stands out.

You get final designs for every screen, state and visual element.

04

Development & QA

Engineering with attention to detail. QA runs alongside development from the very start, setting a high standard early.

Testing covers responsiveness, scalability, resource usage and stability.

05

Launch & grow

A smooth release, performance monitoring, support and maintenance. We handle the App Store approval process for you.

Analytics planned in advance plus user feedback tell us what to prioritise next.
