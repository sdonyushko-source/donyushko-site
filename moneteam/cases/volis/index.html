Introduction

Move data, not people.

The Ministry of the Interior commissioned VOLIS in 2009 with EU funding: councils should be able to meet and take legally binding decisions from anywhere, and residents should see and shape them. Aram, M-One’s founder, built the system end to end. It ran for 17 years in Estonia, was piloted in Sweden and the Netherlands, and closed on 1 July 2026.

Solutions

One system for the whole decision: from the draft to the vote, the protocol and the resident watching it.

01. A whole sitting in one system

From the agenda to the published decision, nothing leaves VOLIS and nothing goes back to paper.

  1. 01

    Preparation

    Agenda, drafts and documents sent to members before the sitting.

  2. 02

    Attendance

    Members check in, in the hall, electronically or virtually.

  3. 03

    Agenda

    The agenda is accepted by vote.

  4. 04

    Debate

    Speech requests, amendments and the draft’s final text.

  5. 05

    Vote

    Open, by name or secret, signed with the national digital ID.

  6. 06

    Protocol

    Written automatically as the sitting runs.

  7. 07

    Published

    Decisions, protocol and video go public.

02. Every vote, in the room and online

The hall screen shows each member’s vote in real time, wherever they are taking part from.

03. Democracy you can watch

Residents follow the sitting live: video, the agenda and every voting result.
