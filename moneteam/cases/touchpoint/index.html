Introduction

Textmagic wanted a standalone product that lets marketers and small teams create on-brand email campaigns in seconds — without design or coding skills.

Textmagic is a business messaging platform. Many of its customers also needed email campaigns, and building them meant hours in third-party builders. M-One took Touchpoint from idea to launch as one team: product design, front-end, back-end, AI integration, DevOps and QA.

Solutions

A chat, an editor and a brand kit — one product from a prompt to a ready email.

01. Describe the campaign. Get a ready email.

A chat on the left, the result on the right — preview and code side by side.

02. Every block stays editable

Text, font size, colours and links change by hand — or the AI rewrites a block on request. No code needed.

03. Paste a website — get the brand

Touchpoint pulls the logo, colours, company description, keywords and social links from a URL. Every new email follows them.
