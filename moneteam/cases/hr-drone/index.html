Introduction

HR Drone is ours — built, launched and run by M-One since 2023.

Candidates get jobs matched to their skills, take skill tests checked by AI and stay anonymous until they choose to show themselves. Recruiters publish jobs, see the best matches and talk to candidates in one place.

Solutions

AI does the screening; people make the decisions.

01. Jobs matched by AI

The best matches for your profile, and a notification the moment a company looks at you.

02. Skill tests checked by AI

Short tests on real tools; a good result can be shown to companies or shared.

03. Anonymous until you choose

04. The recruiter’s side

AI screening of CVs, new messages, open jobs and a to-do list on one dashboard.

05. Talking to candidates

Chat with every candidate, with notes and the job next to the conversation.
