Introduction

Krisp’s Voice AI improves digital voice communication: noise cancellation, accent conversion, call transcription, summaries and more.

It works across all audio hardware and every app that supports voice calls. M-One built the Krisp mobile app for iOS and Android from one codebase — meetings, recordings, transcripts and AI notes on the phone.

Project challenges

Meetings happen everywhere — the notes had to be on the phone, not only at the desk.

01

Meetings on the go

Recordings and notes had to be one tap away on the phone.

02

Two platforms, one team

iOS and Android had to ship together from one codebase.

03

Long calls, short time

An hour-long call had to turn into points you read in a minute.

04

Any work account

Sign-in had to work with Google, Microsoft, Apple, email or SSO.

Solutions

A meetings list, a player with the transcript and AI notes — the whole call in the pocket.

01. Every meeting in one list

Upcoming calls from the calendar with a one-tap Start, and every recording with its notes right below.

03. Key points after every call

Krisp writes the key points of the meeting, and the timeline shows who spoke, when and for how long.

04. Signing in takes one tap
