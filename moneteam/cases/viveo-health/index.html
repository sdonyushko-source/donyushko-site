Introduction

Viveo connects doctors and millions of patients worldwide.

Every doctor can sign up and see patients remotely by video or meet them in person. M-One designed and built the patient apps, the doctors’ web workspace and the back-office.

Solutions

From finding a doctor to paying for the visit — on the phone. The doctor’s whole day — on one screen.

01. Find a doctor and book a visit

Medical experts with ratings and prices, free slots for online and offline visits.

02. Describe the problem before the call

03. The visit itself

A video consultation, a chat with the doctor and a request the doctor receives at once.

04. Everything after the visit

Documents, prescriptions with dosage and the doctor’s signature stay in the patient’s notes.

05. The doctor’s day on one screen

Today’s consultations, patients in the waiting room, invitations and the doctor’s wallet.

06. A consultation in the browser
