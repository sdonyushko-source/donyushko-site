Screened with HR Drone,
decided by people.

HR Drone, our own AI recruiting product, runs the first passes. It reads each CV, sorts every claim into proven or only stated, and places the candidate on our level scale from Intern to Senior 3. Then it drafts an interview built to test that reading.

Reads for evidence

A CV line counts only when the work behind it can be checked.

Blind to background

Name, photo, age, gender, nationality and university are ignored.

Humans decide

The AI writes a recommendation. A lead engineer runs every interview and the Head of Engineering makes every hire.

A task with
a hidden answer key.

Each task has known gaps planted in it. A junior usually finds about two, a mid-level engineer three or four, a senior five or six. We count what they find, fix and admit.

01

We run it

Every submission is built and used, with screenshots of what breaks.

02

“What is still wrong with this?”

Every README must answer it. Honest answers score higher than polished ones.

03

AI is allowed

Candidates may use any AI tool. We test whether they understand what they shipped, and we give their written project brief to a fresh AI agent to see if it can continue the work.

04

Two scores, never averaged

Technical skill and judgment are scored separately. Strong opinions with weak code is still a no.

05

Questions are welcome

Asking good questions during the task counts in the candidate’s favour.
