More about our process →

Discovery callDay 1

We dig into your idea, goals and constraints under NDA, and agree on what success looks like.

AI turns the call into a first scope and we estimate it the same day.

Scope and teamDay 3

You get a written technical assessment: what we’d build, the architecture we’d recommend and why, what it costs, and the team who’d do it.

Matched by HR Drone, our own AI product. By stack and industry, not availability.

KickoffDay 5

We join your tools — your repo, your board, your sprint cadence, your definition of done. No parallel process to manage.

Every pull request gets AI code review and generated tests. The risks worth raising early get raised early.

First demoDay 10

Something you can click. Working product running in test environment, demonstrated live.

Not a progress report, not a slide. From there, a demo every week until launch.

ScaleAfter launch

Add people in days when something takes off. Scale back when it doesn’t.

And everything we build is documented as we go, so you could take it in-house tomorrow if you wanted to.
