About

Vera Sudzilouskaya is a painter born in Minsk, Belarus, and based in Barcelona.

She has trained in classical drawing, painting and composition since childhood, at the Minsk Art Lyceum, Minsk College of Art and the Academy of Arts in Minsk. Later she studied digital and motion design in Vilnius and film direction in Valencia.

  • BornMinsk, Belarus
  • BasedBarcelona, Spain
  • SpeaksEnglish, Spanish, Russian, Belarusian

2018 · Solo exhibition and VR installation, Minsk

“Exit In”

A series of paintings, each with its own three-dimensional virtual reality world. Wearing VR headsets, viewers could step inside the canvases, explore the depth of the painted space and find hidden elements behind the surface of the work.

Role: Artist and curator
